Reel 1 · Footwear · Egypt · 2020 & 2026

Normandy Shoesمعاك من أول خطوة

A premium men's leather brand since 1998, relaunched with one idea: the first step. As Head of Creative Services in 2020 I built the "Footstep Friend" big idea. In 2026 I came back to direct the relaunch end to end: a teaser film cut on the beat of a footstep, a golden art-direction lock, and two months of daily content for 13 branches.

Every visual was produced with generative AI from real product references, so the shoes are always the real shoes. That is the playbook I can bring to any footwear or fashion brand in the Kingdom.
